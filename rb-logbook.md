Section 3C. Optics tells how to align the plano-convex lenses (which act as collimators) with respect to the lamp and the detector to get the most parallel light. 

To finely align the polarizers: place two linear polarizers into the path. Rotate the first to 45°. Rotate the second (close to 135°) until the detector signal is minimal. Then, insert the ¼-wave plate before the second linear polarizer. Rotate (close to 0°) until the signal is maximal. If the light is perfectly circularly polarized, then rotating the second linear polarizer now will not change the signal. (This is what the second polarizer is for!)
